# Code of Conduct

PerlOnJava is a technical project. Everyone who participates in its issues,
pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces is expected to follow
this code.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Judge contributions on technical merit.
- Help newcomers, and assume good faith.
- Keep discussions on topic.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the maintainer through the contact details on
the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/fglock). Reports are kept
confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and other
contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently
restrict a participant's access to project spaces.

## Attribution

version 2.1.
